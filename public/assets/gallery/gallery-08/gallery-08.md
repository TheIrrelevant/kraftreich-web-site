# Deliverables
deliverables:
  - Designed the Monolith residence as a solo architectural concept.
  - Modeled the core architecture with AutoCAD and Autodesk 3ds Max before rendering through Midjourney.
  - Developed exterior, cave threshold, living, pool, rave, work, bedroom, and bathroom scenes as one spatial system.
  - Used Midjourney Edit to refine materials section by section, selecting concrete, glass, water, stone, and interior texture treatments separately.
  - Re-rendered corrected detail passes and converted the architectural world into a cinematic video presentation.
tools: "AutoCAD, Autodesk 3ds Max, Midjourney"
deliverablesIntro: >
  Monolith is a solo architectural concept for a cliffside concrete residence carved into
  a coastal rock formation. The project explores a living environment where mass, shadow,
  glass, sea, and water are treated as one continuous spatial atmosphere.
deliverablesExtra: >
  The workflow was intentionally experimental. The building was modeled in AutoCAD and
  3ds Max, then rendered through Midjourney rather than a conventional render engine.
  Material corrections were handled in parts through Midjourney Edit: concrete, cliff,
  glass, pool water, interior surfaces, and lighting details were selected, reworked, and
  rendered back into the main visual system before the project was turned into video.

# Team
team: []
teamNarrative: >
  This was a solo concept work covering architectural design, art direction, 3D modeling,
  material refinement, image generation, and video visualization.
teamAttribution: "Solo concept work"

# Problem & Solution
summaryStatement: >
  Monolith turns a luxury coastal residence into a heavy, ritual-like concrete object
  embedded in the cliff rather than placed politely on top of the landscape.
problem: >
  A sea-view residence can easily become decorative: glass box, luxury surface, postcard
  view. The challenge was to create a stronger architectural identity where the building
  feels excavated from the site, with interiors shaped by mass, shadow, water, and the
  pressure of the cliff.
solution: >
  The design uses concrete as mass and atmosphere, not finish. The exterior is embedded
  into the rock, while interiors open toward the sea through controlled glass planes and
  deep thresholds. Each room was treated as a spatial condition: cave entry, panoramic
  living room, pool chamber, rave room, work room, and private suites. The experimental
  Midjourney rendering process allowed material and light decisions to be reworked in
  layers, making the final images feel more like a carved architectural world than a
  standard visualization set.
