deliverablesIntro: >
  Street Fashion Architecture is a photography series I shot and edited around the
  relationship between fashion, street, and built space. The work looks for frames where
  a figure, a facade, and urban light become one image instead of a product shot against
  a background.
deliverablesExtra: >
  Some frames lean into fashion presence on the street; others pull architecture forward
  through concrete, relief, and light cuts. Cars, plazas, and city horizons enter as part
  of the same street language. The edit keeps contrast, texture, and posture tight so the
  series reads as one photographic attitude.

# Team
team: []

# Problem & Solution
summaryStatement: >
  Street Fashion Architecture holds fashion and city space in one photographic frame —
  posture, material, and architecture sharing the same street pressure.
problem: >
  Fashion photography often flattens architecture into backdrop, while architectural
  photography often removes the body. On the street, both are already entangled: clothing,
  gesture, wall, light, and city depth arrive together.
solution: >
  The series shoots that entanglement directly. Fashion is framed through posture and
  material against stone, concrete, graffiti, and plaza scale. Architecture is read through
  light slits, relief, and urban depth. Editing keeps the tone consistent so street fashion
  and architectural atmosphere stay one visual language.
