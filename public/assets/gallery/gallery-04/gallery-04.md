# Problem & Solution
summaryStatement: >
  iPad concept sketches keep architectural and landscape ideas alive as drawn thought —
  fast, atmospheric, and open enough to explore before production.
problem: >
  Design ideas can die too early when every thought is forced into polished rendering.
  Without a sketch layer, massing, planting, and atmosphere get locked before they have
  room to breathe.
solution: >
  These iPad drawings treat sketching as a thinking method. Line defines structure,
  wash builds depth, and color marks planting and material mood. The series holds
  concept fragments — house, street, garden, terrace — as visual notes that can grow
  into later design work without losing the first spark.
