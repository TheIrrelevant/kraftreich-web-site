deliverablesExtra: >
  The process combined 3D modeling with experimental AI rendering. The architecture was
  modeled in AutoCAD and 3ds Max, then rendered through Midjourney. Material behavior was
  corrected through Midjourney Edit in separate passes: white stone, glass, water, daylight,
  interior surfaces, and reflection quality were refined in parts before the stills and
  video sequence were generated from the final direction.

# Problem & Solution
summaryStatement: >
  Claritas uses white mass, water, and axial order to create a cultural space where clarity
  feels monumental rather than sterile.
problem: >
  A white architectural concept can easily become empty, over-polished, or generic. The
  challenge was to make clarity feel spatial: to give the building enough weight, shadow,
  reflection, and civic presence while keeping the composition quiet and restrained.
solution: >
  The design is organized around a strong central axis, a reflective water field, and a
  stone volume that reads as both object and threshold. Exterior terraces extend the mass
  into the landscape, while interior scenes use light, scale, and surface discipline to
  hold a cultural atmosphere. The experimental Midjourney workflow made it possible to
  refine material and reflection details independently, giving the final sequence a more
  controlled and cinematic architectural presence.
