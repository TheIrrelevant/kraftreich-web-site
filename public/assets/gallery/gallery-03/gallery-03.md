# Problem & Solution
summaryStatement: >
  Yangi O'zbekiston Park turns a 100-hectare territory into a civic landscape where water,
  planting, promenades, and monument space hold public life at city scale.
problem: >
  A park of this size can easily break into disconnected zones: monument plaza, lakes,
  planting belts, and recreation areas with no shared spatial language. Without a clear
  landscape structure, the territory risks reading as infrastructure and symbolism rather
  than a place people can move through, rest in, and inhabit daily.
solution: >
  The landscape design builds continuity through water systems, planted edges, walkable
  promenades, and open-space sequences that link recreational life to civic axes. Lakes
  and channels organize the park body; planting creates seasonal atmosphere and shade;
  plazas and paths support gathering and movement. Visualization and motion work made
  that landscape readable as one public park experience before and after construction.
